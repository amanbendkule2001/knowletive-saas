## 1. Introduction to the Parent Portal

The **Knowletive Parent Portal** (`/portal`) keeps families closely connected to their children's educational journey. Parents can monitor daily classroom attendance, review examination performance, inspect fee invoices and receipts, and receive direct school notices.

---

## 2. Login & Family Account Setup

- **Login URL:** `/login`
- **Credentials:** Registered parent email and password (Demo: `parent@sunrise.test` / `Admin@123`).
- **Automatic Redirection:** When a parent logs in, the platform links the session to the `Guardian` profile and routes directly to `/portal`.

---

## 3. Multi-Child (Ward) Switcher

If you have more than one child enrolled in the school:
- A **Child Selector Dropdown** appears at the top of your portal.
- Select between your children (e.g., switch from "Aarav Sharma - Grade 10" to "Ananya Sharma - Grade 6").
- The entire portal instantly updates to display the selected child's academic data, attendance, and fee ledgers.

---

## 4. Monitoring Child Attendance & Absence Alerts

- View your child's monthly attendance calendar.
- Receive instant absence alerts whenever your child is marked absent by a teacher.
- Review total days attended, excused absences, and late arrivals.

---

## 5. Tracking Academic Performance & Report Cards

- **Term Results Overview:** Track your child's academic trajectory over multiple terms.
- **Detailed Subject Breakdown:** Inspect raw marks, maximum scores, subject grades, and faculty remarks.
- **Official Report Card Download:** Download and print certified term report cards bearing the institute's verification mark.

---

## 6. Fee Statements, Invoices & Payment Receipts

- **Fee Invoices:** Review all tuition, lab, transport, and term invoices issued for your child.
- **Breakdown of Charges:** Itemized fee heads detailing exactly what is being billed.
- **Payment History:** Chronological log of all fee payments made, including payment method, date, and receipt number.
- **Official Receipts:** View and print signed receipts for tax and reimbursement purposes.

---

## 7. Receiving School Circulars & Direct Alerts

- Read announcements regarding school schedules, parent-teacher conferences (PTM), weather holidays, and extracurricular activities.

---

## 8. Data Privacy, Security & Guardian Verification

- **Strict Family Privacy:** You can only view records for children legally linked to your account via `GuardianLink`.
- All financial and academic data is encrypted and strictly isolated from public access.

---

## 9. Parent FAQs & Support Inquiries

- **Q: One of my children is not showing in my portal.**
  - *Answer:* Contact the school Front Desk or Admissions office. They will verify your guardian record and link your child's profile in `/app/parents/[id]`.
- **Q: Can both mother and father have separate login accounts?**
  - *Answer:* Yes. The school can create multiple guardian profiles (Father, Mother, Local Guardian) all linked to the same student.
