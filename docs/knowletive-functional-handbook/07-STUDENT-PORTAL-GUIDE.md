## 1. Welcome to the Student Portal

The **Knowletive Student Portal** (`/portal`) is your dedicated academic center. It allows you to track your daily attendance, view published examination results, download official report cards, and stay informed on campus announcements.

---

## 2. Login & Access Credentials

- **Login URL:** `/login`
- **Credentials:** Provided by your school administration (Demo: `student@sunrise.test` / `Admin@123`).
- **Automatic Portal Redirection:** When you sign in, Knowletive detects that your account is linked to a student profile and routes you straight to `/portal`.

---

## 3. Your Student Dashboard (`/portal`)

Your dashboard features:
- **Academic Header:** Your Full Name, Admission Number, Roll Number, Class, Section, and Enrolled Batch.
- **Attendance Percentage Card:** Your current attendance standing (e.g., `94.2%`).
- **Recent Examination Results:** Quick summary of your latest published grades.
- **Latest School Announcements:** Important circulars posted by your teachers or principal.

---

## 4. Checking Your Daily Attendance

- Click on **Attendance** in the portal navigation.
- **Calendar Register:** View your day-by-day attendance history:
  - **Green Indicator:** Present.
  - **Red Indicator:** Absent.
  - **Amber Indicator:** Late or Excused.
- Check your overall percentage to ensure compliance with school examination eligibility requirements (e.g., minimum 75% required).

---

## 5. Viewing Examination Report Cards

- Click on **Examinations / Results**.
- Displays cards for all completed terms (e.g., "Term 1 Mid-Term Examination 2026").
- **Card Summary:** Total Marks Obtained, Maximum Possible Marks, Overall Percentage, and Letter Grade (e.g., `A+`).

---

## 6. Detailed Exam Result Snapshot Viewer (`/portal/results/[id]`)

Clicking **View Official Result** opens the frozen official snapshot:
- **School Header:** Official institute name, address, and crest.
- **Subject-by-Subject Breakdown:**
  - Subject Name (e.g., Mathematics, Physics, English).
  - Maximum Marks vs Marks Obtained.
  - Subject Grade & Grade Point.
  - Teacher Remarks.
- **Term Result Summary:** Total marks, grade rank, and official publication timestamp.
- **Print / PDF:** Click **Print Report Card** to produce a clean physical copy.

---

## 7. School Notices & Campus Circulars

- Stay updated with school holidays, sports events, and examination timetables in the **Notices** section.

---

## 8. Data Privacy & Access Safeguards

- **Strict Student Isolation:** You can only view your own grades, attendance, and records. You cannot view the records of any other student.
- **Read-Only Portal:** You cannot alter attendance or grade records.

---

## 9. Student FAQs & Account Assistance

- **Q: Why can't I see my exam results yet?**
  - *Answer:* Results are only visible after the school administration officially publishes them. If your exam is still being evaluated, check back once published.
- **Q: My attendance was marked absent on a day I was present.**
  - *Answer:* Contact your class tutor or batch teacher to review and update the attendance register.
